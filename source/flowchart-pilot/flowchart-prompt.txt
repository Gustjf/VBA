Read Part 1 of that file (the comment lines at the top) and follow it exactly. In short:
1. Reply with one complete .tex file in one code block, from \documentclass to \end{document}. Nothing before it; after it at most two lines of notes.
2. Copy Part 2 of the file (everything from \documentclass to \begin{document}) exactly as it is. Do not shorten, reorder or improve it. Do not add packages, \node, \draw, coordinates or sizes.
3. Write only the content lines between \begin{document} and \end{document}, using only the commands Part 1 lists: \ChartTitle, \ChartSubtitle, \Summary, \KeyBox, \Station, \DocStation, \Gate, \SideDocument, \Loop and the title block fields.
4. Keep the limits: 2 to 6 stations, 2 to 6 bullets per station, up to 12 words per bullet, headings of 2 to 4 words. Facts, not prose. No emoji. Write a percent sign as \%.
5. A gate goes under a station that is not the last one; no gates under neighbouring stations; a side document and the station it feeds have no gate.
6. If my notes are unclear, choose the most sensible reading and say so in one line after the code block. Do not ask me questions first.
